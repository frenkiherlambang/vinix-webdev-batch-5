Product Requirement Document (PRD)Feature Name: Module 5 – Canteen Pre-Order & CheckoutAuthor / PM Role: Technical Lead PM (Campus Life Web App)Status: Ready for Engineering ReviewTarget Release: Sprint 5.11. Executive Summary & Problem Statement1.1 Context & BackgroundUniversity students face tight schedules where transition intervals between consecutive lectures are as short as 15 minutes. Traditional canteen ordering requires queuing twice: once to order and settle cash payments (often slowed down by change shortages), and once to wait for preparation. As a result, students routinely skip meals.1.2 Target PersonaName: Maya (20), 2nd-year undergraduate student.Core Need: Quickly grab lunch between consecutive classes without queuing or carrying physical cash.Pain Points: 15-minute inter-class window; long cash checkout queues; frequent delays due to vendors lacking exact change.1.3 Goals & Success MetricsPrimary Objective: Reduce total transaction and waiting friction to under 3 minutes upon arriving at the stall.North Star Metric: Median Time-to-Pickup (Order Placement $\to$ Counter Collection) $< 5$ minutes.KPIs:Payment completion rate $\ge 92\%$ within the active payment window.Payment gateway status latency $\le 1.5$ seconds post-settlement.Reduction in reported cash-change stall delays by $80\%$.2. User Story & Core Journey2.1 User StoryAs a college student with short class breaks,I want to pay for my canteen order directly via an integrated e-wallet QR code,So that I can pick up my meal immediately at the stall counter without standing in the cash queue.2.2 End-to-End User FlowTray Review: Student inspects items in cart, reviews subtotal, applied campus discounts, and tax/fees.Checkout Initiation: Student taps "Pay via QRIS / E-Wallet"; backend creates an order record and renders a dynamic QR code accompanied by a 5-minute countdown timer.Payment Execution: Student scans the dynamic QR code using GoPay, OVO, or Dana on their mobile device.Real-time Status Transition: Payment gateway fires a webhook; client UI updates instantly from UNPAID to PAID via WebSockets/SSE.Pickup Token Display: System assigns and displays a bold 3-Digit Pickup Token (e.g., #104) with stall details, which the student shows at the counter to claim food.3. Scope Definition3.1 In ScopeDynamic E-Wallet QR Generation: Dynamic QR (QRIS standard compatible with GoPay, OVO, and Dana) embedded with exact payable amounts and order ID.5-Minute Countdown & Expiration Engine: Real-time visual countdown timer; auto-expires and transitions orders to CANCELLED_EXPIRED if unfulfilled at $T = 00:00$.Instant Reactive Payment State Update: Bidirectional communication channel (WebSocket / Server-Sent Events) to push settlement confirmation to the UI without page refreshes.Counter Pickup Token: Auto-generated 3-digit cyclic/stall-scoped token (001–999) rendered upon transaction confirmation.3.2 Out of ScopeBill splitting among multiple students/accounts.Gratuity/tipping system for stall staff.Advance meal scheduling (e.g., ordering for tomorrow or future dates).Direct settlement via Campus Student ID RFID / NFC balances.In-app food delivery to campus classrooms.4. Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)Performance & Latency:Dynamic QR render latency: $< 1.2\text{ s}$ from checkout tap.Webhook-to-Client screen update latency: $< 800\text{ ms}$.Availability & Reliability:$99.9\%$ uptime during campus peak hours ($11:00\text{ AM} - 2:00\text{ PM}$).Webhook listener must be idempotent (using unique transaction references) to eliminate duplicate token generation.Usability & Mobile Optimization:Token screen must feature ultra-legible typography (minimum 48pt font for the 3-digit token) readable from 1.5 meters across a canteen counter.Screen keep-alive/wake-lock enabled on the pickup token screen to prevent phone display sleep while waiting.7. Open Questions & Future ConsiderationsVendor Dashboard Sync: How does stall staff mark the 3-digit token as "Collected" (merchant tablet app vs. physical scan)?Refund Policy for Expired Orders: Standard automated refund flow if an e-wallet deducts funds after an order expires due to network latency.Phase 2 Expansion: Integration with student campus meal plan cards and scheduled pre-ordering for upcoming class days.
